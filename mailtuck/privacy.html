Privacy Policy

Mailtuck · sellerkit · Version 1.1, effective 27 August 2026

Mailtuck saves the email you have open in Gmail™, together with its attachments, into a Notion database you choose. This page states exactly what it touches, where it sends it, and how it is protected.

1. We run no server and keep no copy of your mail

Mailtuck runs inside Google Apps Script under your own Google account. Your email goes from Google to Notion, and, only if you switch on the optional column filling described in section 4, to Anthropic. It never passes through a server belonging to us, because we do not operate one. We hold no copy of your messages, so there is nothing on our side to breach.

2. What Mailtuck can read

  • Only the message you currently have open, via Google's gmail.addons.current.message.readonly permission. This is a narrow, contextual permission: it does not grant access to your mailbox, your search history, or any other message.

Mailtuck does not request permission to read your whole mailbox, your Google Drive, your contacts, or your calendar. Those permissions are absent from its manifest, so it cannot use them even if asked to.

3. What Mailtuck writes to Notion

When you press Save, it creates one page in the Notion database you selected, containing the subject, the message text, and the attachments. Attachments larger than your Notion plan's file limit are not uploaded; a line naming the file is written instead, so nothing disappears silently.

4. The optional AI feature, and exactly what it sends

Mailtuck Pro can fill your Notion database's own columns from the email. That feature, and only that feature, sends message content to a third-party AI service.

  • It is off unless you turn it on. It does nothing until you paste your own Anthropic API key into the add-on, and you can switch it off again at any time and keep using everything else.
  • Where it goes: directly from Google Apps Script to Anthropic's API at https://api.anthropic.com, over HTTPS. It does not pass through any server of ours.
  • What is sent: the subject, sender, date and the first 6,000 characters of the body of the one message you are saving, plus the names and types of the columns in your chosen Notion database. Attachments are never sent to the AI service.
  • When: only at the moment you press Save, and only for that one message.
  • Whose account: yours. The request is authenticated with your key and billed to your Anthropic account, so your own agreement with Anthropic governs it. Their terms and privacy policy apply to that request.
  • What comes back: suggested column values. Anything that is not already a property of your database is discarded before anything is written.

5. Limited Use of Google Workspace data

Mailtuck's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The use of raw or derived user data received from Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, Workspace user data obtained through Mailtuck is never used, transferred or sold to develop, train, retrain or improve any generalised or foundational machine learning or artificial intelligence model. The AI feature in section 4 uses an existing third-party model to produce an answer for you, at the moment you ask for it, under your own account. It does not contribute your mail to anyone's training data, and we ourselves receive no Workspace data at all, so we have none to train on, transfer, or sell.

6. Your credentials

Your Notion integration secret, and your Anthropic API key if you set one, are stored in Google's per-user property store, readable only by your own copy of the add-on. We never receive either of them. Selecting “Disconnect Notion” deletes the Notion secret immediately, and clearing the key field deletes the Anthropic key immediately.

7. How your data is protected

Mailtuck handles personal and potentially sensitive information: the contents of an email. These are the mechanisms that protect it.

  • Encrypted in transit. Every outbound request is HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or better) to a fixed list of hosts declared in the add-on's manifest: api.notion.com, api.anthropic.com and our licence endpoint. The add-on cannot reach any other host, and plain HTTP is not used anywhere.
  • Encrypted at rest, and isolated per user. Credentials and settings live in Google's Apps Script user property store, which Google encrypts at rest and scopes to a single user of a single script. One user's copy cannot read another's.
  • No data store of ours to protect. We operate no database, no logs of message content, and no backups containing your mail. Message content exists in your Gmail, in your Notion workspace, and in transit between them.
  • Least privilege. The add-on requests three scopes and no more, none of them a restricted scope, and the Gmail scope is the contextual one that exposes a single open message rather than a mailbox.
  • Access control on our side. No employee, contractor or subprocessor of ours can access your Workspace data, because none of it reaches us. The Google Cloud project and the Apps Script deployment are held in a single owner account protected by two-step verification.
  • Bounded exposure. Only the message you are actively saving is read, and the AI feature sends at most the first 6,000 characters of it. Nothing is read in the background and there is no scheduled or bulk access.
  • Deletion on request. Uninstalling removes the stored credentials and settings with them; see section 10.
  • Incidents. If we ever become aware of a security problem affecting users, we will publish it on this site and email affected users where we are able to contact them.

8. No tracking

No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising identifiers, no profiling. We do not sell or share data, because we never receive any.

9. Children

Mailtuck is a workplace tool and is not directed at children under 13.

10. Removing your data

Uninstalling the add-on removes its stored credential and settings with it. Pages already created stay in your Notion workspace, where you control them. If you would like confirmation that nothing of yours remains with us, write to the address in section 12 and we will answer within five business days, noting that we hold no message content in the first place.
